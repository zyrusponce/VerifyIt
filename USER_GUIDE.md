
# VerifyIT User Guide

## Welcome to VerifyIT

VerifyIT is a digital-literacy learning site that helps you recognize misinformation, check online claims, and share information more responsibly. You can begin learning without an account.

## Start Here

Use the navigation bar to move between the main parts of the site:

| Section | What you can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Learn** | Read short lessons about misinformation and complete a knowledge check. |
| **Investigation Lab** | Practice assessing fictional online-content cases using evidence. |
| **Quiz** | Test your knowledge in topic-based quizzes or the Mixed Challenge. |
| **Verification Toolkit** | Follow a practical checklist before sharing a post or claim. |
| **My Progress** | Review your completed activities and quiz results. |
| **Certificate** | Check your eligibility, claim, download, or verify a certificate. |

Use the sun or moon button in the navigation bar to switch between light and dark appearance. On a phone or smaller screen, open the menu button to see all navigation links.

## Learn With Lessons

1. Select **Learn** from the navigation bar.
2. Choose a lesson card to open a topic.
3. Read the lesson content, examples, and key takeaways.
4. At the end, answer every question in the **Knowledge check**.
5. Select **Check my answers** to see explanations for each answer.

Completing a knowledge check marks the lesson as complete even if some answers are incorrect. You can select **Try again** at any time to practise again and improve your score.

## Use the Investigation Lab

The Investigation Lab lets you apply verification skills to sample cases. All cases and social posts shown in the lab are fictional learning materials.

1. Open **Investigation Lab** and select a case.
2. Review the background context, post, and available evidence.
3. Inspect each evidence area and decide whether the claim is trustworthy, misleading, or needs more verification.
4. Choose your assessment and optionally explain which clues influenced you.
5. Select **Submit Investigation**.

After submitting, VerifyIT shows the expected assessment, explains the evidence, highlights clues you may have missed, and suggests next verification steps. Submit an assessment for every case to meet the certificate requirement; your assessment does not need to match the expected answer.

## Take Quizzes

1. Open **Quiz**.
2. Select a topic, such as source credibility, fact-checking, image manipulation, or deepfakes. The **Mixed Challenge** combines questions from multiple topics.
3. Answer each question and continue until the quiz is complete.
4. Review your score, explanations, and recommended lessons.
5. Choose **Try again** to take another attempt.

Your progress page keeps your latest and best quiz scores. Quiz scores are useful for practice but are not required for a certificate.

## Check Content Before Sharing

Open **Verification Toolkit** whenever you see a surprising, emotional, or important claim online.

Work through the **Before You Share** checklist:

1. Find the original source.
2. Identify the author or publisher.
3. Check the publication date.
4. Read beyond the headline.
5. Look for supporting evidence.
6. Compare independent, reliable sources.
7. Check the context of images and videos.
8. Watch for missing context or emotional pressure.
9. Pause before sharing uncertain information.

Tick each step as you complete it. The page also provides links to external verification tools, which open in a new tab. Select **Reset Checklist** when you want to begin again with a new claim.

## Investigate With the AI Assistant

The AI assistant requires you to sign in. Open it from the assistant button or the **AI Assistant** page when available.

1. Paste an article, headline, or social-media post into the text box. Include the headline, author, and date when possible.
2. Optionally enter the original web address and select **Get text from link** to load its text. Review the loaded text before analysing it.
3. Optionally attach screenshots or photos in JPG, PNG, or WebP format. You may also paste or drag images into the workspace.
4. Select **Analyze Content**.
5. Review the summary, extracted claims, potential warning signs, verification checklist, related-source results, and initial assessment.

Treat the analysis as a starting point, not a final ruling. AI can make mistakes, so check the original source and independent evidence yourself. VerifyIT does not save submitted text or images, but they are sent to an AI service to generate the analysis. Do not submit private or sensitive information.

## Track Your Progress

Select the chart icon or **My Progress** to view completed lessons, Investigation Lab cases, and quiz performance. The page identifies strengths and topics to revisit after you take quizzes.

### As a guest

You can complete lessons, quizzes, investigations, and the checklist without registering. Your progress is stored only in the current browser on the current device. It will not appear on another device and may be lost if you clear browser data.

### With an account

Create an account or select **Sign in** to save verified progress to your account and access it across devices. If you completed activities as a guest, choose **Save to my account** from the banner or your account page after signing in. The site will re-check the saved activities before adding them to your account.

If a connection problem delays a save, use **Retry now** when it appears. Avoid clearing guest data on a shared computer until it has been saved to your account.

## Manage Your Account

Open your profile initial in the navigation bar and choose **My Account** to:

- Set or update your display name.
- Check what progress is saved to your account.
- Import progress from the current browser.
- Retry unsent changes.
- Clear local guest data from a shared device.
- Sign out.

Use **Forgot your password?** on the sign-in page to request a password-reset email.

## Claim Your Certificate

To claim a Certificate of Completion, you must:

1. Sign in to an account.
2. Complete every learning module by finishing its knowledge check.
3. Submit an assessment for every Investigation Lab case.

When you meet these requirements, open **Certificate**, confirm the name to print, and select **Claim Certificate**. The name is printed exactly as entered and cannot be changed after the certificate is issued.

After claiming it, you can download a PDF or open its verification page. A certificate recognizes completion of VerifyIT activities; it is not a government, academic, or professional credential.

## Verify a Certificate

Anyone can check a certificate without an account.

1. Open **Verify Certificate**.
2. Enter the certificate number exactly as printed, or open the QR-code link on the certificate.
3. Review the status and the displayed recipient, issue date, and certificate number.

If the site cannot connect while checking a certificate, try again later. A temporary connection error does not mean the certificate is invalid.

## Need Help?

- If a page does not load, check your internet connection and refresh the page.
- If progress is not saved to an account, sign in again and use **Retry now**.
- If you use a shared computer, save guest progress to your account before clearing browser data.
- If a claim cannot be verified, do not share it until you can find reliable, independent evidence.
